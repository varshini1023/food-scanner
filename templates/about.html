ABOUT FOOD SCANNER

Understand your food.
Make informed choices.

Food Scanner is a student-developed project that combines machine learning and nutrition information to help people explore the food they eat and learn more about their health.

🌱 Our Mission

Our goal is to make food and nutrition information easier to explore through technology. By combining image recognition with accessible nutrition information, Food Scanner aims to encourage awareness, curiosity and more informed everyday food choices.

What can you explore?

Four ways Food Scanner helps you learn about food and wellbeing.

📸

Food Recognition

Upload a food image and let our machine learning model predict which of its supported food categories the image most closely matches.

🥗

Nutrition Information

Explore estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats and other nutritional values associated with a predicted food and serving size.

📏

BMI Calculator

Calculate your Body Mass Index using height and weight, and explore general information about the resulting BMI category.

🧠

SPARKS

Discover bite-sized information about the brain, psychology, food, the human body and everyday habits through an interactive learning experience.

How does it work?

A simple process connects a food image with nutritional information.

STEP 01

Upload

Choose a clear image of one of the food categories supported by the application.

STEP 02

Predict

The trained machine learning model analyses the image and predicts a food category.

STEP 03

Explore

View the predicted category and its associated estimated nutrition information.

💜 A note on accuracy

Food Scanner is an educational project, not a medical diagnostic tool. Image predictions may be incorrect, and nutritional values are estimates that can vary with ingredients, portion sizes and preparation methods. The information should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
